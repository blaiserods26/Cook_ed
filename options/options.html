ℹ️ About

Cook_ed is a browser extension that helps you manage cookies and track consent across websites.

Features:

  • Track cookie consent across websites
  • View all stored cookies by domain
  • Automated cleanup of stale cookies
  • Whitelist trusted sites
  • Delete unwanted cookies individually

Version: 1.0.0

Data Storage: All data is stored locally on your device
